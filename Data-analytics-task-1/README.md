## 📋 Project Objectives

✔ Performed exploratory data analysis on the Superstore dataset.

✔ Identified trends, patterns, and relationships between business metrics.

✔ Generated descriptive statistical summaries.

✔ Executed SQL queries to answer business questions.

✔ Built visualizations to support business decision-making.

✔ Designed an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 📊 Business Insights

- Identified sales performance across different regions.
- Evaluated profitability by product category.
- Analyzed the relationship between discounts and overall profit.
- Examined customer segment behavior to understand purchasing patterns.
